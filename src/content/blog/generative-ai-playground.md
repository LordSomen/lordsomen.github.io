## Overview

The Generative AI Playground is designed to bridge the gap between AI research and scalable production systems. It covers:
- **Foundational Concepts**: LLMs, transformers, attention mechanisms, and embeddings.
- **Practical Implementations**: Working code examples using industry-standard libraries.
- **Advanced Techniques**: Vector databases, RAG systems, fine-tuning, and prompt engineering.
- **Real-World Applications**: End-to-end projects combining multiple AI concepts.
- **Educational Notebooks**: Jupyter notebooks with detailed explanations and visualizations.

## Learning Modules

### 1. Generative AI Fundamentals 📚
Core concepts for building generative models including MLP classification, BERT sentiment analysis, full fine-tuning of BERT, token generation, transfer learning with MobileNetV3, and PyTorch/HuggingFace ecosystems.

### 2. Transformers & Attention Mechanisms 🔗
Deep understanding of the transformer architecture with self-attention implemented from scratch, BERTViz for attention bias visualization, custom GPT model creation, and multi-head attention concepts.

### 3. Natural Language Processing 🗣️
Fundamentals and applications covering text tokenization with HuggingFace tokenizers, token encoding and embeddings, text generation from character-level models, and training on the Shakespeare text dataset.

### 4. Diffusion Models 🎨
Generative modeling through diffusion techniques including DDPM (Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models), Diffusers library implementation, image inpainting and outpainting, sketch-based generation, and custom U-Net architecture.

### 5. Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) ⚔️
Architecture and training of basic GANs, StyleGAN implementations, discriminator and generator network dynamics, and adversarial training techniques.

### 6. Vector Databases & Embeddings 🔍
Semantic search and vector storage focusing on basic vector search concepts, embedding functions, multimodal search (text and images), and simple recommender systems using movies/ratings datasets.

## Key Skills Learned
- **Frameworks & Libraries**: PyTorch, HuggingFace Transformers, LangChain, Diffusers
- **Techniques**: Fine-tuning, RAG, Transfer Learning, Attention Mechanisms
- **Models**: LLMs, Vision-Language Models (VLM), GANs, Diffusion Models
