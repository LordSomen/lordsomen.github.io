# Reinvention with Agentic AI

**Completed:** Jul 2024 (Placeholder)  
**Issuer:** Coursera  

## Overview
This certification demonstrates expertise in building and deploying autonomous AI agents that leverage advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) to perform complex workflows.

## Key Learnings & Concepts
- **Agentic Workflows:** Designing AI systems that can plan, reason, and execute multi-step tasks autonomously.
- **Tool Use & Function Calling:** Equipping LLMs with external tools (like APIs, calculators, and search engines) to interact with the real world.
- **Multi-Agent Systems:** Coordinating multiple specialized AI agents to collaborate on complex, overarching tasks.
- **Evaluation & Safety:** Implementing guardrails and evaluation metrics to ensure agents operate safely and deterministically.

## Skills Acquired
- Agentic Workflows
- LLM Integration
- Prompt Engineering
- Autonomous Systems
